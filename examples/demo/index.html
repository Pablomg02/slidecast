syncdeck

HTML presentations with a presenter view,
synced screens and speaker notes.

Press → or Space to advance

How it works

  • One server holds the live step of every deck.
  • The presenter moves it with arrows, a clicker or a phone.
  • Every viewer follows over a WebSocket, within a second.

Three roles

ViewerWatches the deck: the projector, or students on their laptops.
PresenterNotes, next step, timer and controls. Password when remote.
AdminUploads decks and writes notes, at a hidden /admin.

Keys

→ Space PgDnnext step
← PgUpprevious step
B .blank screen
Ffull screen

Make your own

A deck is a folder with index.html and an optional deck.json for notes.

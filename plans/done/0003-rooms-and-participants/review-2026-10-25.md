## Note on running e2e locally

With Node 22.22.1 and Playwright 1.64.1, Playwright's ESM loader breaks on
`content-disposition` (ESM-only, required by `@fastify/static`). Running with
`PW_DISABLE_TS_ESM=1 npx playwright test` works. CI is not affected.
